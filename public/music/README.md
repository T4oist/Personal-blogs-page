# Music

Place your audio files here (MP3 recommended; M4A, OGG, WAV, AAC and FLAC are also listed, playback support depends on the browser).

The filename without its extension becomes the displayed track title, for example `Artist - Song.mp3`.
Tracks are sorted by filename. Rebuild the site after adding or removing files; deploy to publish them for visitors.
Only use audio you are permitted to publish. These files are public when the site is deployed.
